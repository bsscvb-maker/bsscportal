Who can receive a Sister Patch?

  • Women who are full members in good standing with at least 12 continuous months of full membership at the cutoff.
  • Members who meet the club’s attendance standard or have an approved adjustment, and have no active probation or suspension at the cutoff or before presentation.
  • Members who have never received a Sister Patch, including before this policy.

A panel of eligible women members reviews documented service, support for fellow members, follow-through, and positive impact.
