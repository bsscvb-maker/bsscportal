Review the commitments included in your record

Eligibility and term: Your eligibility, election, appointment and term are governed by the adopted bylaws. Full voting membership in good standing, applicable renewal requirements and candidate qualifications must be verified from club records. Signing this acknowledgment does not grant a Board seat or an officer title. Confirm your actual term before signing.

Commitment and attendance: Serve your authorized term, prepare for and attend Board meetings, participate in general meetings, events and fundraisers, and complete assigned work between meetings. Notify the President AND Vice President at least 24 hours before an absence when practicable, or as soon as possible in a true emergency. Attendance exceptions, review and removal follow the adopted bylaws.

Representation and respectful conduct: Uphold the club’s mission and reputation. Act respectfully toward members, officers, partners and the public. Follow meeting procedures and the chair’s authorized directions. Ask questions and disagree constructively without threats, intimidation, personal attacks or disruption. Any corrective action follows the bylaws and fair process.

Responsibilities and practical skills: Review budgets, donations, policies and bylaws before voting. Use the club’s email, phone and approved digital tools. Lead or support assigned committees, projects and events. Report progress, protect records and ask for help before deadlines are missed.

Confidentiality and conflicts: Protect private Board business, member information, finances and other nonpublic information during and after service. Confidentiality does not prevent required disclosures, good-faith reports to appropriate authorities or confidential professional advice. Disclose conflicts of interest and follow the applicable recusal rules.

Financial and nonprofit stewardship: Act in the club’s best interests, follow spending and contract approval limits, keep receipts and protect designated funds. Support nonprofit and tax-exempt compliance. Do not make unauthorized commitments in the club’s name. Complete any conflict-of-interest disclosure required by the Board.

Fundraising and member support: Support fundraising through volunteer time, promotion and practical work. Personal contributions are encouraged when able; no donation minimum is created here. Welcome and mentor members and support membership growth and retention.

Officer selection and handover: Election to the Board does not guarantee a particular office. Officer selection follows the bylaws. Be willing to support the assigned structure and learn your responsibilities. Transfer records, property, access and outstanding work through a documented handover when your role ends.

Governing authority: The adopted bylaws and operating policies control over this acknowledgment. This form does not amend qualifications, terms, authority, voting thresholds or removal procedures. Duties and conduct concerns are reviewed under the bylaws with required notice and a chance to respond.
