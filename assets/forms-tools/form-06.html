Reference template from the October 18, 2026 governance review. This document does not submit or save information. Existing approved rules and working online processes remain in effect.

Use the existing Future Shakers online application. No second enrollment application is needed.

A parent or legal guardian applies for ages 5–17. Participants ages 18–20 apply for themselves. All participants need an eligible sponsoring member.

Submission does not approve enrollment. The Secretary checks the application and the Board decides enrollment. Record the review, decision and subsequent updates in the restricted participant record.
