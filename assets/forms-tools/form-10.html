Distinguish informal counseling and immediate safety action from formal discipline. Formal warnings, probation and membership suspension require the approved Board decision; an officer cannot issue them unilaterally. Do not use an ordinary sanction record to bypass elected-seat or presidential removal. Preserve good-faith reporting and anti-retaliation protections. Record only necessary sensitive facts in general meeting minutes.
