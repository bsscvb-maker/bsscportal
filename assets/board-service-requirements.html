Board Service Requirements and Expectations

For prospective and incoming BoneShakers Social Club directors. Read this before accepting a nomination or Board seat. The adopted bylaws and operating policies control.

Complete Board election acceptance

Eligibility and term

To serve as a director, you must be an eligible voting full member in good standing, have one completed year of active full membership by taking office, satisfy the applicable renewal qualification, demonstrate leadership and commitment, and attend at least 75% of general membership meetings actually held from January 1 through the close of nominations, rounded up. This candidate attendance rule also applies to Lifers and Past Presidents. Appointees are measured through appointment consideration. A documented attendance hardship exception requires the bylaw vote and does not waive the one-year requirement. The Secretary verifies qualifications from official records.

Ordinary elected directors serve staggered two-year terms beginning January 1. Core officers are selected separately by incoming directors. The President serves two years; the Vice President, Secretary, Treasurer and Sergeant at Arms serve one year. Confirm the specific seat and term on the announced ballot and election record.

Your eligibility, election, appointment and term are governed by the adopted bylaws. Full voting membership in good standing, applicable renewal requirements and candidate qualifications must be verified from club records. Signing this acknowledgment does not grant a Board seat or an officer title. Confirm your actual term before signing.

Commitment and attendance

Serve your authorized term, prepare for and attend Board meetings, participate in general meetings, events and fundraisers, and complete assigned work between meetings. Notify the President AND Vice President at least 24 hours before an absence when practicable, or as soon as possible in a true emergency. Attendance exceptions, review and removal follow the adopted bylaws.

Representation and respectful conduct

Uphold the club’s mission and reputation. Act respectfully toward members, officers, partners and the public. Follow meeting procedures and the chair’s authorized directions. Ask questions and disagree constructively without threats, intimidation, personal attacks or disruption. Any corrective action follows the bylaws and fair process.

Responsibilities and practical skills

Review budgets, donations, policies and bylaws before voting. Use the club’s email, phone and approved digital tools. Lead or support assigned committees, projects and events. Report progress, protect records and ask for help before deadlines are missed.

Confidentiality and conflicts

Protect private Board business, member information, finances and other nonpublic information during and after service. Confidentiality does not prevent required disclosures, good-faith reports to appropriate authorities or confidential professional advice. Disclose conflicts of interest and follow the applicable recusal rules.

Financial and nonprofit stewardship

Act in the club’s best interests, follow spending and contract approval limits, keep receipts and protect designated funds. Support nonprofit and tax-exempt compliance. Do not make unauthorized commitments in the club’s name. Complete any conflict-of-interest disclosure required by the Board.

Fundraising and member support

Support fundraising through volunteer time, promotion and practical work. Personal contributions are encouraged when able; no donation minimum is created here. Welcome and mentor members and support membership growth and retention.

Officer selection and handover

Election to the Board does not guarantee a particular office. Officer selection follows the bylaws. Be willing to support the assigned structure and learn your responsibilities. Transfer records, property, access and outstanding work through a documented handover when your role ends.

Governing authority

The adopted bylaws and operating policies control over this acknowledgment. This form does not amend qualifications, terms, authority, voting thresholds or removal procedures. Duties and conduct concerns are reviewed under the bylaws with required notice and a chance to respond. What a normal month looks like

Read the agenda and reports before the meeting. Come ready to ask questions and vote. Between meetings, monitor agreed communication channels and finish assigned work. Allow meeting time plus a few hours a month for preparation and tasks; major fundraisers and officer duties can take more. This is a planning estimate, not an adopted hourly minimum.

Before you say yes

Discuss your actual workload, term and availability with the President. Ask which projects need help and who will guide you. Be willing to do practical work and support decisions made through the proper process. You do not need to know everything on day one, but you do need to learn, communicate and follow through.
